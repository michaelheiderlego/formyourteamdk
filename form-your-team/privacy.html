Privacy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

In short

What you write in the canvas stays in your own browser. I don’t see it, store it or share it. The only time anything is sent anywhere is when you choose to send me a message through the contact form.

Who runs this site

Michael Heider, Denmark. You can reach me at michael.heider321@gmail.com.

Your canvas and answers

Everything you enter (team name, participants, answers to the exercises) is saved only in your browser’s local storage on your device, so you can come back to it later. It is never sent to a server. If you clear your browser data or use a private window, it is gone. When you export a file or download a PDF, that file is created on your device.

Contact form

If you send a message, your message and, if you choose to give it, your email address are sent to me by email through FormSubmit, a form-forwarding service. I use them only to reply to you, and I delete messages when they are no longer needed. You can ask me at any time to see or delete what you have sent.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the page and keep it secure; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

No tracking

There are no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers. Fonts and scripts are served from this site itself, not from third parties.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you, and to complain to a data protection authority. In Denmark that is Datatilsynet.
