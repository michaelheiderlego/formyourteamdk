Privacy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

In short

What you write in the canvas stays in your own browser. I don’t see it, store it or share it. The only time anything is sent anywhere is when you choose to send me a message, sign up for the free playbook or join the course waitlist.

Who runs this site

Michael Heider, Denmark. You can reach me at michael@formyourteam.dk.

Your canvas and answers

Everything you enter (team name, participants, answers to the exercises) is saved only in your browser’s local storage on your device, so you can come back to it later. It is never sent to a server. If you clear your browser data or use a private window, it is gone. When you export a file or download a PDF, that file is created on your device. If you take the video course, your progress and the name for your certificate are saved the same way, only in your browser.

Contact form

If you send a message, your message and, if you choose to give it, your email address are sent to me by email through FormSubmit, a form-forwarding service, and land in my email inbox, which is hosted by Google. Both services may process your message outside the EU, including in the US. I use your message only to reply to you (the legal basis is my legitimate interest in answering, GDPR article 6(1)(f)), and I delete messages when they are no longer needed. You can ask me at any time to see or delete what you have sent.

Free playbook and course waitlist

If you sign up for the free playbook or join the waitlist for the course, your first name (if you give it) and your email address are stored with my email service, MailerLite, a company based in the EU (Lithuania). MailerLite also records when and from which IP address you signed up and confirmed, as proof of your consent. You first get an email asking you to confirm your address; nothing else is sent until you do.

I use your details to send you what you asked for: the playbook and the two free lessons, or the link when the course opens. If you also tick the optional box for notes on team development, I add you to a separate list for those. If you don’t tick it, you won’t get them. MailerLite tells me whether emails are opened and which links are clicked, so I can see what is useful. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can ask me at any time to see or delete your details.

The legal basis is your consent (GDPR article 6(1)(a)). I keep your details until you unsubscribe or ask me to delete them.

Buying the course

When the course is for sale, payment is handled by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the seller (merchant of record). Your name, email address, billing country and payment details go to Lemon Squeezy, not to this site; see Lemon Squeezy’s privacy policy. I receive your name, email address and order details so I can give you access and help if something goes wrong.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub, a US company, processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the page and keep it secure, which may happen outside the EU; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

Videos

The intro videos, the free lessons and the course videos are hosted on this site itself. Playing them sends nothing to YouTube or any other video service.

Visitor statistics

To understand which pages and materials are useful, this site uses Umami, a privacy-friendly analytics tool. It sets no cookies and doesn’t identify you or follow you across other sites. It counts page views and a few anonymous events, such as downloads, video plays, sign-ups and clicks on “Join the waitlist”, together with general information like country, browser and device type.

There are no cookies, no advertising trackers and no social media plugins. Fonts, videos and scripts are served from this site itself. Links to LinkedIn, Miro and other sites are plain links: nothing is sent to them unless you click.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you, to get a copy of it, to object to how I use it, and to withdraw your consent at any time (this doesn’t affect what happened before). To use these rights, email me at michael@formyourteam.dk. You can also complain to a data protection authority. In Denmark that is Datatilsynet.
