Form your team

Six conversations every team needs. A free workshop guide by Michael Heider.

The six conversations

  • Why are we here? Purpose, beneficiaries and a shared picture of success.
  • Who are we? Strengths, working preferences and personal connection.
  • How do we work? When we plan, make and review, and how we give feedback.
  • How do we make decisions? Twelve ways to decide, and which one to try.
  • How do we want to be together? A practical team contract: what is in, and what is out.
  • How do we learn and reform? Keep, change, try, and when to revisit the canvas.

Each conversation has key questions, exercises to run in the room and fields to capture what you agree. Answers save in your browser, with no account.
